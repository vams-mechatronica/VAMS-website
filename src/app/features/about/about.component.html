Who we are

A DPIIT-recognized technology company

VAMS Mechatronica specializes in Industrial IoT (IIoT), machine connectivity, automation systems, predictive maintenance and MES integrations. Our flagship platform, ProMonitor, brings real-time visibility by connecting CNC machines, PLCs, robots and shop-floor equipment into one platform.

We work across railways, defence, automotive, engineering, molding, textile and heavy manufacturing, and deliver solutions that are secure, scalable and tailored to each customer’s operations.

What we do

From machine signal to decision

Connect

Machine connectivity for CNC machines, PLCs, robots and sensors over open industrial protocols.

Monitor

Real-time and production monitoring so operators and management see the same live picture.

Improve

Predictive maintenance and analytics that help prevent failures and plan work from machine data.

Our platform

ProMonitor

We develop and implement industrial digital solutions on our own platform, and provide access to the development environment. ProMonitor is available as SaaS (cloud) or on-premise, with an open REST API and a live data stream for connecting existing IT systems.

We are also developing an AI-based job scheduler to support day-to-day planning and help prevent failures.
