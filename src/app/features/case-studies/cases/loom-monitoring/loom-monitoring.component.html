IoT-Based Loom Monitoring

Real-time loom intelligence implemented for a textile manufacturing facility to improve productivity, reduce breakdowns, and enhance fabric quality.

Client Overview

The client operates a large-scale textile manufacturing unit with multiple weaving looms running continuously across shifts. Consistent machine performance and fabric quality are critical to meet delivery commitments and quality standards.

Operational Challenges

  • Frequent loom stoppages due to yarn breakage
  • No real-time visibility into spindle speed and runtime
  • Manual production tracking with delayed reporting
  • High fabric defects caused by undetected process variations
  • Reactive maintenance leading to production losses
Loom Performance Trend Graph

Solution Implemented

An IoT-based loom monitoring system was deployed to collect real-time operational data, detect anomalies, and provide actionable insights to production and maintenance teams.

Real-Time Loom Monitoring

Continuous tracking of spindle speed, loom runtime, stoppages, and operating status across all machines.

Yarn Breakage Detection

Instant alerts generated on yarn breaks to enable rapid operator intervention and minimize fabric defects.

Production Analytics

Real-time production counts, efficiency metrics, and shift-wise performance visibility.

Dashboards & Visualization

  • Spindle speed trends and stoppage analysis
  • Yarn breakage frequency and root cause patterns
  • Shift-wise productivity and efficiency metrics

Conclusion

The IoT-based loom monitoring solution enabled the textile manufacturer to gain real-time visibility into loom operations, significantly reduce breakdowns, and improve both productivity and fabric quality. The system supports continuous improvement and scalable expansion across additional looms and plants.
