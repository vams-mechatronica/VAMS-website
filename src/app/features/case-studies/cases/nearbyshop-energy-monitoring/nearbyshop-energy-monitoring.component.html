Smart Energy Monitoring for Process Plant

Real-time energy intelligence implemented for an FMCG process plant (NearbyShop) to optimize consumption, eliminate outages, and improve operational efficiency.

Client Overview

NearbyShop operates a high-throughput FMCG process plant with multiple production lines running continuously. Energy consumption forms a significant portion of operational costs, making efficiency and reliability critical to business performance.

Operational Challenges

  • Lack of real-time visibility into energy usage across production lines
  • Undetected load imbalances and peak demand spikes
  • High energy costs due to inefficient consumption patterns
  • Risk of unplanned outages affecting production continuity
  • No centralized energy monitoring or analytics platform
Energy Consumption Trend Graph

Solution Implemented

A comprehensive Smart Energy Monitoring solution was deployed across multiple production lines to measure, analyze, and optimize energy consumption in real time.

Energy Metering Network

Intelligent energy meters installed at feeder, line, and machine levels to capture granular energy data.

Real-Time Analytics

Continuous tracking of voltage, current, power factor, demand, and load patterns across all production lines.

Alerts & Protection

Automated alerts for overloads, phase imbalance, and abnormal consumption trends to prevent outages.

  • Peak demand and load profile visualization
  • Energy cost analysis by shift and process
  • Power quality monitoring (Voltage, PF, Harmonics)
